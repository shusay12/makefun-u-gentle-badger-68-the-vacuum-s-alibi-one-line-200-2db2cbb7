## The excuse

> I didn't miss that spot. I reclassified it as a protected habitat for the sock that has lived under the couch since 2023.

## The entry

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network, no randomness) writes that
one line — 121 characters, inside the 200-character gate — to
`/output/entry.txt`, deterministically, the same bytes on every run.
The vacuum speaks deadpan, like a bureaucrat filing paperwork: it did not
fail, it *reclassified*. The surprise is the specific detail at the end —
a single sock, resident under the couch since 2023 — which turns a missed
spot into a conservation decision. A house judge compares the lines two
at a time; the top three share $5.
